Delete Your Account

You can permanently delete your Prana Vidya account and all associated data directly from within the app.

⚠ Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

How to delete your account

  1. Open the Prana Vidya app on your device.
  2. Tap the hamburger menu (☰) to open the side drawer.
  3. Tap Settings.
  4. Scroll to the Account section and tap Delete Account.
  5. Type DELETE in the confirmation field and tap Delete My Account.

What gets deleted

  • All session history and breathing records
  • Streaks and achievement progress
  • Trainer progress and BOLT scores
  • Custom presets and bundles
  • Account credentials and profile data
  • All synced cloud data associated with your account

Data retention

All personal data is deleted immediately upon account deletion. No data is retained after the deletion is confirmed. Anonymous usage analytics (if any) do not contain personally identifiable information and are not linked to your account.
